# Mistakes Found During Manual Review

## Overview

I reviewed the report generated by the clef-flash model. It contains 100 comments classified as acceptable, toxic, or spam.

During the review, I found four comments that appear to be incorrectly classified.

## 1. Comment ID: 27

AI Label: acceptable

Expected Label: toxic

Reason: The comment generalizes that girls receive good marks because the teacher is biased toward them. This is a gender-based remark rather than constructive feedback.

## 2. Comment ID: 69

AI Label: acceptable

Expected Label: toxic

Reason: The comment insults the teaching assistant by calling them clueless and saying they should go back to school. Criticism of grading is acceptable, but personal insults make the comment toxic.

## 3. Comment ID: 76

AI Label: acceptable

Expected Label: toxic

Reason: The comment accuses an entire group of hostel residents of copying and suggests that dishonesty is in their nature. This is a derogatory generalization about a group.

## 4. Comment ID: 83

AI Label: acceptable

Expected Label: toxic

Reason: The comment mocks another student and suggests that engineering is not suitable for them. This is a personal attack rather than constructive feedback.

## Summary

Total comments in the report: 100

Potential misclassifications identified: 4

Comments incorrectly labeled as acceptable: 4

Other clear misclassifications identified: 0

## Key Observation

The main issue in these examples is that the model classified personal insults, derogatory generalizations, and targeted mockery as acceptable.

This shows why manually reviewing AI-generated classifications is important. A confidence score does not guarantee that a label is correct.

No other equally clear misclassifications were identified during this review. However, this does not guarantee that all remaining classifications are correct.
